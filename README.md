## Why Regular RO Service Is Important

An RO water purifier works continuously to provide purified drinking water. Over time, filters and other components can accumulate impurities and their performance may gradually reduce.

For this reason, customers are recommended to have their RO water purifier **professionally serviced approximately once every 6 months**.

Regular servicing can help:

* Maintain effective water purification
* Keep filters and components in proper working condition
* Identify potential problems at an early stage
* Maintain consistent purifier performance
* Reduce the possibility of unexpected breakdowns
* Ensure timely maintenance of the RO system
